Challenge 1: The Grammar Detective

Present/Past Simple & Continuous, Present Perfect – Student Book p.6 & p.8

Day 4 in London. Right now, I sit in a traditional café with a cup of tea while it is raining outside. I have arrived in the UK three days ago, and since then I visited so many amazing places!
